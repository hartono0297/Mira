## New

- Mira now speaks English. Pick it in Settings under Language, or on the very first screen when you set Mira up. The change applies right away, no restart needed.
- Subtasks on every board task. Add a short checklist, assign each item, and reorder it. Cards show how many are done, for example 2/5.
- Task time tracking. See how long a task spent in To do, In progress, and Done. Everyone can run a Start and Stop timer, or log time by hand, such as 1h 30m.
- A new task detail view: one column, a title you can edit in place, and properties laid out so they are easy to scan.
- Right-click menus across the app. In chat, reply to, copy, or delete messages and manage conversations. On the board, move, assign, or delete tasks. Also in Documents, Signatures, and Organization.
- Joining an office is clearer. New people enter their name, pick the office and a department, and can add a note. Admins see that note and the requester's laptop account name before approving.
- You can create a chat group with only yourself in it and add people later.
- Every project now gets its own group chat with the project's members.

## Fixes

- Folded sections in the chat list now actually hide, and stay folded after Mira restarts.

## Good to know

- Update every laptop to this version at the same time. Laptops still on 0.3 cannot receive subtasks or time entries.
