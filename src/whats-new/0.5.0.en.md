## New

- Join with your office Windows account. On a PC signed in with a domain account, Mira picks the office for that domain and fills in your name and department from Active Directory. Check the details, then send your request.
- Join request cards check Active Directory again: the account exists and is active, and the name and department match. An admin still clicks Approve.
- Superadmins can set the office's AD domain and match OUs to Mira departments in Organization, under Departments.
- One domain, one office. Mira no longer creates a second office for the same domain, and lets you know when laptops on the network belong to a different Mira office.
- Offices whose laptops still run an older Mira now show up during setup, with the steps to update them.
- Clearer task details: everything you can change has a field frame, and read-only details show as plain text.

## Good to know

- MacBooks and PCs without a domain account join as before: enter your name, pick the office and department, then wait for approval.
- Details from your Windows account (account name, name, department) are kept in the office records and are visible to office members.
- Joining with AD details turns on once every laptop in the office runs this version.
