## New

- Divisions inside a department. A department admin creates divisions, such as Mobile or Web, and appoints their division heads. Division heads add and remove the people in their division. One person can be in several divisions.
- Each division has its own chat under Departments in the sidebar, right below its department channel.
- Department channels and division chats can have a pinned note and announcement mode. In announcement mode only the people who manage the chat can post, and everyone else can still read.
- A My department tab in Organization for department admins and division heads: divisions, members, join requests, invite codes, projects, and department channel settings in one place. Department admins can also make other people in their department admins.
- New projects can belong to a division through the Division option. The people in that division join the project right away.
- Emoji reactions on anyone's message. Click a reaction to add yours, click it again to take it back, and point at it to see who reacted.
- An emoji picker in the message box, searchable in Indonesian or English. Type a colon and a name to get suggestions.
- Office stickers. A superadmin adds them in Organization, under Stickers. Everyone can send them as stickers, or write :name: in a message to show one as a small emote inside the text.
- Personal stickers. Keep a sticker from a colleague's message with Save sticker in the right-click menu, or upload your own image in the Mine tab.
- Chat now shows all of a person's skill labels, including your own on your messages.
- Coffee, a way to say thanks. Choose Give coffee in the right-click menu of a colleague's message or on their profile, or press Thank (name) on a task that is Done. Add a short reason.
- Coffee also comes from work: finishing a task a colleague assigned to you, or finishing a task on time. Coffee moves your level from Newcomer to Regular, Senior, and then Legend.
- The Coffee tab in Organization shows the Most helpful this month board and how to earn coffee. A superadmin can turn Coffee off for the whole office, or turn off only the monthly board.
- Profile photos. Set yours in Settings, under Profile, or from your profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar, then adjust the crop. Colleagues see your photo in Chat, Board, and Organization.
- Task timers now have Pause and Resume. Start the timer, pause it with one click, and resume whenever you like. The task details header shows your total time on that task.
- Your timer pauses on its own when you start a timer on another task, in any project, and when the task moves to Done or is deleted. A notice appears above the board.
- A Calendar menu: tasks with due dates in Month, Week, or Agenda view. Filter by My tasks or by project. Drag a task to another day to change its due date, or use Alt+Arrow keys and the right-click menu.
- The sidebar can collapse to icons only, with the button below the logo or Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on Mac). Your choice is saved on this laptop.
- Deactivate a member without removing them. A deactivated person gets no new messages or changes. Once reactivated, they use the same laptop and return to the departments, projects, and labels that still exist, without a restart. Superadmins and department admins (for regular members of their department) can deactivate and reactivate.
- Remove for good is still there for superadmins. A laptop removed for good gets an explanation and a Reset and start over button, so it can ask to join again.
- OU mappings in Organization can name a division. People approved through AD join that division automatically.

## Fixes

- When joining, Mira now explains below the department choice why your Windows account details were not used: the office domain is different, the office domain is not set yet, some office laptops have not been updated, or your AD department has not been matched by an admin yet.
- If the office has no AD domain yet, a superadmin signed in with a domain account is offered that domain. Superadmins can also match an OU to a department right from a Join request card.
- A removed laptop is no longer stuck on a screen where Check again seems to do nothing. The screen explains what happened and shows the result of every check.
- A timer left running when Mira closes or the laptop sleeps no longer keeps counting until Mira opens again. Mira pauses it at the time Mira was last active.

## Good to know

- Update every office laptop to 0.6.0 at the same time. Creating divisions and deactivating members turn on once every laptop in the office runs this version. The My department tab and the Deactivate dialog list the laptops that still need the update.
- Laptops still on Mira 0.5 don't see reactions, Coffee, profile photos, or office stickers. On those laptops a sticker message shows as :name: with the image as an attachment, and colleagues' photos show as initials.
- Once the office has deactivated someone, join requests from laptops with an older Mira can't be approved. Install 0.6.0 on new laptops first.
- Logging time manually is hidden for now. Existing manual entries still show, still count, and can still be deleted by their owner.
- Everyone in the office can see the reason for a coffee. Each person can give up to 3 coffees a day. Guests can receive coffee but can't give it.
- Profile photos are visible to every office member, Guests included. Mira strips location data and other metadata from a photo before saving it. A superadmin can remove a member's photo.
- Reactions don't send notifications.
- Mira ships without built-in stickers. Each office builds its own collection.
- The calendar only shows tasks that have a due date.
- Reset and start over deletes that office's data on the laptop, such as messages, chat files, and documents. Signed PDFs you already exported, your language, and your theme stay.
